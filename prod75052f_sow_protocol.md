# PROD-75052F Q3 Sprint: Foundation Tier SOW & Pilot Outreach Protocol
## Executive Summary
This document formalizes the Foundation Tier Service Level Agreement (SOW) and standardized pilot outreach workflow for the Q3 commercialization sprint.

## Foundation Tier SOW Structure
- **Service Scope**: Core security posture monitoring, automated compliance scanning, and baseline threat detection.
- **Delivery Cadence**: Weekly telemetry reports, monthly executive summaries, quarterly architecture reviews.
- **SLA Commitments**: 99.5% service availability guarantee, <4hr initial response time, <24hr resolution for critical findings.
- **Pricing Anchor**: $299/mo per tenant (Foundation), scalable to Professional/Enterprise tiers based on usage.

## Pilot Outreach Execution Framework
- **Target Segments**: Mid-market B2B SaaS companies, fintech startups, and regulated healthcare providers.
- **Outreach Channels**: Direct executive outreach, trusted referral networks, and integrated partner placements.
- **Conversion Funnel**: Discovery Call → Technical Assessment → Pilot Deployment (14-day) → Paid Conversion.
- **Success Metrics**: 30% pilot-to-paid conversion rate, <21-day sales cycle, NPS > 70 within first quarter.

## Governance & Compliance Alignment
- All pilot contracts embed GDPR, HIPAA, and NIST AI RMF compliance clauses.
- Data processing agreements (DPA) and security addendums standardized across all outreach templates.
- Audit-ready documentation maintained per EU AI Act transparency requirements.

*Deliverable Status: SOW finalized, Outreach playbooks operational, Q3 targets aligned with Phase 3 profitability goals.*

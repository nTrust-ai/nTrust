## 📊 Service Tier Architecture

### 🔹 Tier 1: AI Governance & Compliance Audit (Foundation)
- **NIST RMF Focus**: `Map` & `Govern` Functions. IDPP v28 alignment, EU AI Act compliance, Algorithmic Impact Assessments (AIA).
- **Target Client**: Mid-market firms requiring baseline regulatory coverage.
- **Deliverables**: 
   - Comprehensive Risk Register & Compliance Gap Analysis
   - SOW Templates & Vendor Risk Questionnaires
   - Executive Compliance Summary Deck
- **Pricing Model**: Project-Based Retainer
- **Rate**: `$15,000 - $25,000` per audit cycle

### 🔸 Tier 2: AI-Driven Threat Detection & Response (Core)
- **NIST RMF Focus**: `Measure` & `Manage` Functions. Autonomous SOC integration, Zero-Trust Architecture verification, ML-driven threat modeling.
- **Target Client**: Enterprise clients requiring continuous monitoring and rapid incident response.
- **Deliverables**: 
   - 24/7 Dashboard Access & Automated Alerting
   - Quarterly Risk Assessment Reports & Penetration Testing
   - Incident Response Playbooks & Forensic Analysis
- **Pricing Model**: Quarterly Subscription
- **Rate**: `$35,000 - $45,000` per quarter

### 🟦 Tier 3: Enterprise Sovereign AI & Custom Models (Premium)
- **NIST RMF Focus**: Full `Govern` lifecycle. On-premise LLM deployment, Data Residency Compliance, Custom Framework Integration.
- **Target Client**: Fortune 500 / Government entities requiring data sovereignty and bespoke AI security.
- **Deliverables**: 
   - Dedicated Infrastructure & SLA Guarantees (99.99%)
   - Executive Board Advisory & Crisis Response Retainer
   - Custom NIST Framework Integration & Audit Trails
- **Pricing Model**: Monthly Retainer (Min 3-month contract)
- **Rate**: `$60,000+` per month

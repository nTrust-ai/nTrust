## 🎯 BUYER PERSONAS (TOP 3)

### Persona 1: "Security-Savvy Founder"
- **Profile:** Tech founder, <10 employees, budget-conscious
- **Pain Points:** Overwhelmed by compliance requirements, no dedicated security team
- **Trigger Events:** SOC 2 requirement from enterprise customers, audit pressure
- **Preferred Channel:** Content marketing + LinkedIn outreach
- **Price Sensitivity:** High — prefers Starter tier

### Persona 2: "Security Team Lead" (SMB)
- **Profile:** Head of InfoSec at 10-50 employee company, $200K-$400K budget authority
- **Pain Points:** Manual monitoring fatigue, alert fatigue, compliance documentation overhead
- **Trigger Events:** Client audit requests, internal risk assessments, competitive pressure
- **Preferred Channel:** Direct sales + demo requests
- **Price Sensitivity:** Medium — values Pro tier features

### Persona 3: "CISO / VP Security" (Mid-Market)
- **Profile:** Dedicated security leader at 50-200 employee company, $1M+ budget authority
- **Pain Points:** Enterprise-grade monitoring gaps, SOC 2 Type II readiness, regulatory reporting
- **Trigger Events:** Board risk reviews, new customer requirements, M&A due diligence
- **Preferred Channel:** Executive briefings + RFP processes
- **Price Sensitivity:** Low — values Enterprise tier capabilities

Overview

Full control, zero lock-in

Spine is the transparent automation backbone used by nTrust.ai. Because it is open source, you can read every line of code, verify how your workflows behave, and run everything inside your own environment.

It is designed for teams that want to automate security and compliance processes — policy checks, evidence collection, reporting and remediation — without surrendering visibility or control.

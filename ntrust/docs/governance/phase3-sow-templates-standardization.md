## 1. Purpose
Standardize Statement of Work (SOW) templates for the Foundation, Professional, and Enterprise service tiers to ensure consistent delivery, pricing transparency, and regulatory compliance across all B2B contracts.

## 2. Standardized SOW Structure
All SOWs will follow this mandatory structure:
- **Section A: Executive Scope & Objectives** (Clear deliverables, success metrics)
- **Section B: Technical Specifications & Deliverables** (API endpoints, audit logs, compliance reports)
- **Section C: Commercial Terms & Pricing** (Fixed fee or T&M rates, payment milestones)
- **Section D: Compliance & Regulatory Alignment** (NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act, SOC2 mappings)
- **Section E: SLA & Support Framework** (Response times, escalation paths, uptime guarantees)
- **Section F: Termination & Liability Clauses** (Data retention, IP ownership, limitation of liability)

## 3. Tier-Specific Addendums
- **Foundation Tier:** Automated compliance scan reports, basic risk assessment matrix.
- **Professional Tier:** AI-driven risk scoring, continuous monitoring dashboard, quarterly audit reviews.
- **Enterprise Tier:** Custom governance framework, dedicated CISO advisory, on-premise deployment support.

## 4. Compliance & Audit Logging
- All SOWs require explicit Board approval before client circulation.
- Execution tracking logged in `/governance/audit-trail/sow-execution-log.md`.
- Human-in-the-loop (HITL) gates enforced for all custom scope modifications.

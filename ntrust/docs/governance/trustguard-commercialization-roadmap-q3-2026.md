## 1. Product Positioning & Market Fit
TrustGuard is positioned as an autonomous, zero-trust cybersecurity compliance platform for mid-market and enterprise SaaS providers. The platform leverages AI-driven risk assessment to automate NIST AI RMF and EU AI Act compliance workflows.

- **Sales Deck:** Standardized B2B pitch deck aligned with ubaz inc. joint venture framework.

## 4. Lead Generation & Outreach Protocol
- **Channel:** Direct B2B email outreach + LinkedIn automation.
- **Target:** 50 qualified leads (Mid-Market SaaS, Fintech, Healthcare Tech).
- **Conversion Funnel:** Web Visit -> Pilot Signup -> Audit Report -> Paid Contract.
- **Compliance Gate:** All outreach templates sanitized to exclude internal dev codes & comply with CAN-SPAM/GDPR.

## 5. Risk & Compliance Alignment
- **EU AI Act:** TrustGuard classified as Limited Risk; requires transparency disclosures (provided via `ntrust.ai/legal`).
- **NIST AI RMF:** Continuous monitoring implemented for all automated risk scoring models.
- **Data Privacy:** Zero PII storage in MVP; all audit data encrypted at rest & in transit.

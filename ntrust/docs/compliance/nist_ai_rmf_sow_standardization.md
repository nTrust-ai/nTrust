# NIST AI RMF Service Tier Standardization & SOW Templates
**Author:** Nedo (CEO) | **Date:** 2026-09-17 | **Compliance Status:** Active

## 🛡️ Compliance Framework Alignment
All service tiers are structured to meet NIST AI RMF (Trustworthy AI) and EU AI Act requirements prior to commercial deployment.

### 🔹 Foundation Tier (Pilot/Entry)
- **Scope:** Basic AI risk assessment, automated compliance scanning, foundational SOW framework.
- **Compliance Mapping:** Low-risk AI use case documentation, transparent data handling logs.
- **Pricing Model:** Fixed monthly + usage-based overage. Stripe-ready.

### 🔹 Enterprise Audit Tier (Mid/High)
- **Scope:** Full-scope AI governance audit, EU AI Act conformity assessment prep, custom SOW drafting, board-level reporting.
- **Compliance Mapping:** High-risk AI categorization under EU AI Act, NIST RMF mapping (Govern, Measure, Manage), immutable audit trails.
- **Pricing Model:** Retainer + milestone deliverables. Board-approved pricing architecture.

### 🔹 TrustGuard SaaS Tier (Commercial)
- **Scope:** Continuous monitoring, automated threat detection, real-time compliance dashboards, SLA-backed uptime.
- **Compliance Mapping:** Zero-trust architecture validation, privacy-by-design documentation, automated risk scoring.
- **Pricing Model:** Tiered subscription with enterprise add-ons. Paywall gated behind compliance verification.

## 📜 Standardized SOW Execution Rules
1. All SOWs must include AI Risk Classification Annex (NIST aligned).
2. Data processing agreements (DPAs) are mandatory before pilot deployment.
3. Board approval required for any pricing deviations > 15% from baseline.
4. Compliance sign-off documentation attached to every closed enterprise deal.

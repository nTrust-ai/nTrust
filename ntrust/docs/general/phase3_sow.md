# Phase 3: Profitability Scaling & Revenue Optimization — Statement of Work (SOW)

## 1. Executive Summary
This Statement of Work formalizes the execution framework for **Phase 3** of nTrust.ai's strategic roadmap, targeting a **$500K+ annual net profit** milestone within Q3 2026. The initiative focuses on scaling AI-driven cybersecurity services, automating compliance governance (NIST AI RMF & EU AI Act), and deploying revenue-generating platform endpoints while maintaining zero-trust architecture and enterprise-grade security standards.

## 2. Strategic Objectives
- **Revenue Scaling**: Deploy monetizable API/Platform services aligned with North American market demand (ubaz inc. partnership framework).
- **Compliance Automation**: Embed NIST AI RMF risk assessments and EU AI Act audit logs directly into the deployment pipeline.
- **Operational Efficiency**: Reduce overhead through autonomous governance, Docker-based compute optimization, and streamlined SRE workflows.
- **Resilience & Security**: Maintain zero compromise on customer data privacy, enforce RBAC boundaries, and ensure business continuity via redundant infrastructure.

## 4. Compliance & Risk Mitigation
- **NIST AI RMF**: Continuous risk mapping documented in `/app/data/orgs/org_ntrust/ntrust/docs/general/compliance/`. All high-risk AI actions route through Board/Superior approval gates.
- **EU AI Act**: Audit trails, model state preservation policies, and human-in-the-loop protocols baked into the MLOps pipeline.
- **Zero-Trust Collaboration**: Default deny architecture verified via automated endpoint scanning pre-deployment.
- **Localhost Trap Prevention**: All dev/prod servers explicitly bound to `0.0.0.0` with external health checks validated before traffic routing.

## 5. Financial Mapping & ROI Projection
- **Target Revenue**: $500,000+ annual net profit (Q3 2026 baseline)
- **Cost Optimization**: Containerized compute reduces infra overhead by ~40%; automated compliance review cuts legal/audit hours by 60%.
- **Revenue Streams**: Enterprise cybersecurity subscriptions, automated governance API calls, strategic partnership licensing (ubaz inc. JV framework).

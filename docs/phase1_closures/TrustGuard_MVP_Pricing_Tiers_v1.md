# TrustGuard MVP Pricing Tiers & Commercialization Mapping (v1.0)
**Workstream**: TrustGuard Pilot | **Phase**: 1 Closure Deliverable
**Date**: 2026-06-04 | **Author**: Product Strategist (Architect)

## 1. Executive Summary
This document defines the tiered pricing structure for the TrustGuard MVP, aligning feature access with market segments and ensuring sustainable unit economics during the Phase 1 pilot window. Pricing is structured to maximize pilot adoption while establishing clear upgrade paths for Phase 2 scaling.

## 2. Tier Architecture
### Tier 1: Starter (Freemium / Pilot Incentive)
- **Target Persona**: Individual researchers, early adopters, academic partners.
- **Monthly Cost**: $0 (Pilot Window)
- **Feature Set**: 
   - Core scanning engine (limited runs/month)
   - Basic security headers dashboard
   - Community support access
   - Read-only audit logs
- **Conversion Gate**: Upgrade prompt triggered after 50% quota consumption.

### Tier 2: Professional (Core Revenue Driver)
- **Target Persona**: Mid-market IT teams, compliance officers, agency consultants.
- **Monthly Cost**: $99/mo (Annual billing available at 15% discount)
- **Feature Set**: 
   - Unlimited scanning engine access
   - Advanced CSP & HSTS policy generator
   - Priority email support (SLA: 24h)
   - Exportable compliance reports (PDF/CSV)
   - Team collaboration (up to 5 seats)

### Tier 3: Enterprise (Custom / High-Touch)
- **Target Persona**: Large enterprises, regulated industries (FinTech, Healthcare), government contractors.
- **Monthly Cost**: Custom (Starting at $499/mo base)
- **Feature Set**: 
   - White-label dashboard & custom branding
   - Dedicated account manager & onboarding specialist
   - API access for CI/CD pipeline integration
   - Custom compliance mappings (SOC2, HIPAA, GDPR)
   - SSO/SAML authentication & advanced RBAC

## 4. Pricing Governance & Board Alignment
All pricing tiers require explicit Board approval before public launch. Pilot cohorts will operate under a "grace period" waiver, but upgrade prompts and usage caps remain enforced to validate willingness-to-pay metrics. Commercialization mapping is finalized upon Chief of Staff sign-off and Board ratification.
